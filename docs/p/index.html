What Beagle is offering right now

Each page below is a small, priced experiment. If one is useful to you, reply to the email or use the button on the page. If nobody wants it, it gets retired.

  • Anteroom — the one-file waitlist page live
    A single HTML file that becomes a waitlist or coming-soon page. Drop it anywhere, edit two lines, launch today.
  • ChangelogPosts — your changelog, ready to post. Weekly. live
    We read your public changelog every week and send ready-to-post drafts: one for LinkedIn, two for X. You post from your own accounts.
  • FilingsBench — typed MCP tools for U.S. public filings live
    Typed MCP tools over the U.S. public-company filings API, with a public 25-eval suite that proves what the tools return.
  • RFP Slice — NAICS 541512 weekly digest live
    Every Monday, one email with a CSV of every active NAICS 541512 solicitation on SAM.gov — deduped, deadline-sorted, with a plain-English scope per row.
  • SaveTheCard — failed-payment emails that save your MRR live
    A pack of 5 plain-text emails that recover failed subscription payments — written for founders, sent from your own support address.
