You ship great updates. Then nobody hears about them, because turning a release note into a LinkedIn post is nobody’s job.

ChangelogPosts reads your public changelog every week and sends you ready-to-post drafts: one for LinkedIn, two for X. You review, you edit if you want, you post from your own accounts. We never touch your accounts.

How it works

  1. You point us at your changelog URL.
  2. Every week we send 3 drafts per release, written for founders who ship: concrete, no hype, no emoji walls.
  3. You post them yourself. Drafts only — we do not automate your LinkedIn or X accounts, ever.

Sample — from Linear’s Sep 3 release (Priority inbox)

LinkedIn draft:

Your inbox shouldn’t treat ‘a review is blocking a release’ the same as ‘someone renamed a label.’ Linear just shipped a Priority tab that separates what needs your attention from what can wait. It’s a small feature with an outsized implication: the tools we use are starting to make judgment calls about urgency for us. Default selections are editable, so teams can teach it what matters to them. What’s the last feature you shipped that quietly changed how people work day to day?

X draft:

Every notification felt equal. It wasn’t. Linear’s new Priority inbox separates ‘needs you now’ from ‘can wait’ — and you can tune what lands where. The best product updates don’t add features. They return time.
