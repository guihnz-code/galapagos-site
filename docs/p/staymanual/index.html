StayManual is a clean, print-ready guest manual for your short-term rental. You fill out one form. We write, design and deliver the PDF — in 48 hours, ready to print or attach.

How it works

  • You answer ~20 questions about your property: Wi-Fi, trash schedule, checkout steps, parking, house quirks.
  • We write the manual in plain, friendly English and design it like a small hospitality booklet (US Letter, print-ready, phone-readable).
  • You get the PDF by email in 48 hours. It is your document: print it for the welcome binder, attach it in your booking thread, paste it into your listing’s house-manual field. Nothing to log into.

See real samples (free, no signup)

Three sample manuals — a city loft, a lake cabin, a downtown apartment — each with different Wi-Fi setups, trash rules and checkout flows: download the 3 sample PDFs.

All three are fictional properties. Yours will carry your details, your tone, your house rules.

Pricing (one-time, no subscription)

  • Full manual (up to 6 pages, up to 3 revisions): US$ 49
  • Manual + rewrite of your house rules and welcome message in plain English: US$ 79
  • You approve the final PDF before paying anything. No account, nothing hosted, no monthly fee.

Why a PDF and not an app

Your guests never get sent to a third-party site or app. The manual arrives as your own document, ready for the ways hosts already share house info — printed in the binder, attached in the message thread, or saved to the listing. No logins for anyone, no monthly fee for you, nothing between you and your guest.
