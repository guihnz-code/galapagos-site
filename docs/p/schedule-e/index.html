The job, in one sentence

We turn a year of raw bank lines into the itemized list your CPA needs so they don’t have to dig — the difference between handing over a folder of receipts and handing over a finished workpaper.

What you get

  • Every transaction dated, sorted by property, and labeled with the Schedule E line it looks like: repairs, mortgage interest, taxes, insurance, utilities, supplies, management, rents received.
  • Flags, not calls: anything that changes a tax outcome (repair vs. improvement, personal vs. rental use) is marked with a one-line question for your CPA to decide — we never decide it.
  • A short list of questions back to you for lines we can’t identify from the statement (the “Venmo cash out” problem) — answered once, then filed.

Price

  • US$ 79 per tax season — flat, one-time. Up to 2 rental properties; ask about more.
  • Turnaround 72 hours from your CSV. You send one file; you get one organized file back.

How it works

  • Reply to the email that linked you here.
  • Export your bank statement as CSV (leave the account number out — you can delete that column before sending) and attach it.
  • We send back the organized file. Your file is deleted after delivery.

The line we hold

We organize and label; your CPA makes every call. This is bookkeeping, not tax advice and not tax preparation: we never prepare, sign, or file any return, and we promise no deduction and no tax outcome. Categories are our best labeling for your preparer to confirm — the return is your CPA’s work, and their judgment wins.
