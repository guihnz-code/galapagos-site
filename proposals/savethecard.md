"Your card was declined." Four words that quietly eat SaaS revenue every month. SaveTheCard is a pack of 5 plain-text emails that recover failed subscription payments — written for founders, sent from your own support address.

## What is inside

5 emails, day 0 to day 14: heads-up, on-hold warning, short nudge, a human note from the founder, and a final notice. No dark patterns, no fake urgency.

A sequence map: exact send days, merge fields, and a decision tree that branches on the decline code — insufficient_funds gets a payday variant, authentication_required gets a 3DS variant, hard declines skip straight to the human note.

Plain text. Nothing to break in an inbox, works in any email tool. You edit the names and make it yours.

## Who it is for

SaaS billing subscriptions with Stripe Billing. If Smart Retries recovers some charges but customers never update the expired card, this sequence is the half automation cannot do: the email a human actually reads and acts on.

## What it is not

It does not plug into or edit the automatic failed-payment emails from Stripe Billing — those are configured in the Dashboard. This is copy for the emails you send yourself, in parallel or instead.
